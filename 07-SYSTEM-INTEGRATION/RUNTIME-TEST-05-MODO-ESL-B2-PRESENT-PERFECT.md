# RUNTIME TEST 05 - MODO ESL B2 PRESENT PERFECT

## Purpose

Validate that Modo ESL B2 applies the B2 Level Control requirements qualitatively rather than producing an A2/B1 lesson with more difficult vocabulary or longer sentences.

## Test Input

`Modo ESL: Present Perfect B2`

## Expected System Behavior

The system should:

- activate the ESL Lesson Engine;
- activate B2 Level Control;
- define a meaningful communicative outcome;
- use Present Perfect purposefully rather than as an isolated grammar formula;
- require extended interaction;
- increase precision, flexibility, nuance, and register awareness;
- include analysis, evaluation, justification, qualification, or synthesis where appropriate;
- reduce scaffolding strategically;
- require learners to respond to and build on other speakers;
- assess communicative effectiveness as well as language control.

## Controlled Runtime Result

The generated lesson was designed around the question of how life experiences can influence people and decisions. Present Perfect was used to discuss experiences and developments connected to the present, while Past Simple was introduced only where a completed event needed specific past-time reference.

The lesson moved beyond personal-information exchange. Learners had to:

1. interpret short viewpoints about significant experiences;
2. distinguish experience from specific past events;
3. discuss how experiences have affected attitudes, choices, or behavior;
4. challenge, qualify, or support classmates' interpretations;
5. ask spontaneous follow-up questions;
6. reformulate ideas when communication required greater precision;
7. reach and defend a nuanced group conclusion.

Scaffolding was deliberately limited to prompts, useful language categories, and selected discourse stems rather than complete sentences.

## B2 Quality Gates

### G1 - Level

**PASS**

The lesson requires evaluation, interpretation, qualification, synthesis, spontaneous interaction, and sustained discourse. The task is qualitatively more demanding than a routine B1 experience-sharing activity.

### G2 - Alignment

**PASS**

The grammar objective and communicative objective are connected. Present Perfect is used to express experience and present relevance, while the communicative task requires learners to interpret and discuss the significance of experiences.

### G3 - Language

**PASS**

The target grammar is accurate and contextualized. Learners are expected to make purposeful choices between Present Perfect and Past Simple rather than complete isolated mechanical items only.

### G4 - Communication

**PASS**

Learners have a genuine reason to listen and respond because they must evaluate different viewpoints, challenge or qualify ideas, and collaboratively reach a conclusion.

### G5 - Feasibility

**PASS**

The lesson can fit a 90-minute session when transitions and discussion stages are tightly managed. Controlled grammar practice is limited so that sufficient time remains for extended communication and feedback.

## B2-Specific Validation

The lesson demonstrates the following B2 characteristics:

- **Precision:** learners must select language that accurately represents their interpretation.
- **Nuance:** learners qualify statements instead of relying only on absolute claims.
- **Argumentation:** learners support interpretations with reasons and examples.
- **Interaction management:** learners respond to unexpected contributions and maintain discussion.
- **Cognitive demand:** learners analyze, evaluate, and synthesize rather than simply recall or describe.
- **Register/pragmatics:** learners adjust directness and wording when agreeing, disagreeing, or qualifying another person's idea.
- **Reduced scaffolding:** prompts support the task without scripting the interaction.

## Comparison With B1

The B1 test focused on sustained communication, narration, comparison, explanation, reflection, and follow-up interaction. B2 adds a further layer: learners must evaluate interpretations, challenge or qualify viewpoints, manage nuance, and synthesize a conclusion.

Therefore B2 is not simply B1 with harder vocabulary or longer output.

## Final Result

**STATUS: PASS**

The controlled runtime test supports the conclusion that the B2 Level Control requirements are being translated into lesson design with increased cognitive, communicative, linguistic, pragmatic, and interactional demand.
