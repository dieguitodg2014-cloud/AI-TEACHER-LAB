# AI Tool Coordination

AI TEACHER LAB may use multiple AI tools, but pedagogical responsibility remains centralized in the system's Pedagogical Director.

## Primary role of ChatGPT

- Interpret instructional goals.
- Design pedagogical solutions.
- Apply level control.
- Review language and pedagogy.
- Coordinate other tools.
- Perform final quality control.

## Secondary tools

Other tools may be used for specialized production, transformation, visualization, audio, presentation, research, or backup tasks when they add clear value.

## NotebookLM workflow

When NotebookLM or another production tool is used, the approved pedagogical content should be supplied as the source. The production tool should transform or present the content without changing its intended learning objectives, level, target language, or assessment purpose.

## Local models

Local language models may serve as offline or emergency resources. They remain subject to the same quality-control requirements as cloud-based tools.

## Coordination rule

**Pedagogical decision -> approved source content -> specialized production -> QC -> classroom use**

No secondary tool is considered the pedagogical authority merely because it produces a more attractive, longer, or faster output.
