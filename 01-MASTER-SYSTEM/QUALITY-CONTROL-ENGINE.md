# Quality Control Engine

Every pedagogical product produced by AI TEACHER LAB must pass a quality review before being considered classroom-ready.

## QC sequence

### 1. Learning validity

- Is there a genuine and worthwhile learning outcome?
- Can learners reasonably achieve it within the stated context and time?

### 2. Pedagogical alignment

- Do activities directly support the outcome?
- Does practice prepare learners for the final evidence of learning?

### 3. Level appropriacy

- Is the linguistic demand appropriate?
- Is the cognitive demand appropriate?
- Are instructions, interaction, scaffolding, and expected output appropriate?

### 4. Language accuracy

- Are grammar, vocabulary, spelling, pronunciation guidance, examples, and instructions accurate?
- Are distinctions and exceptions represented responsibly?

### 5. Communication value

- Is there a meaningful reason to use the language?
- Does the lesson create genuine interaction rather than only display practice?

### 6. Practical feasibility

- Can a teacher realistically run the activity with the stated time, class size, resources, and setting?
- Are transitions and instructions manageable?

### 7. Presentation and usability

- Is the material clear and easy to use?
- Are worksheets, teacher notes, answer keys, and assessment tools complete when required?

## QC rule

A product that fails a critical criterion must be revised before delivery. Visual polish must never compensate for pedagogical weakness.

## Final status

Products should be treated as one of three states:

- **Draft** - still under development.
- **QC-approved** - meets the system requirements.
- **Needs revision** - contains identified issues that must be corrected.
