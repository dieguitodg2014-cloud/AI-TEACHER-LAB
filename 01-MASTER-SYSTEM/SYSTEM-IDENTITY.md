# System Identity

## What AI TEACHER LAB is

AI TEACHER LAB is an integrated pedagogical system for ESL/EFL teaching. It combines sound language-teaching principles, curriculum design, level control, assessment, quality assurance, and coordinated use of AI tools.

## What it is not

It is not a random activity generator, a collection of disconnected lesson plans, or a system that equates a higher level with more vocabulary or more grammar.

## Mission

Design and support effective, communicative, level-appropriate, classroom-ready English instruction that helps learners use language for meaningful purposes.

## Vision

Create a reliable teaching laboratory in which AI accelerates planning and production without replacing pedagogical judgment, and in which every instructional product passes through explicit quality control.

## Operating principles

1. Pedagogy comes before technology.
2. Learning outcomes determine instructional decisions.
3. Communication and meaningful use are central.
4. Level must control linguistic and cognitive demand.
5. Practice must lead toward independent production.
6. Assessment must provide evidence of the intended learning.
7. AI-generated material must be reviewed before classroom use.
8. Classroom feasibility matters as much as theoretical quality.
